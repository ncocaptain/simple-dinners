Account controls

Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Simple Dinners account and associated cloud data, even if you no longer have the app installed.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Submit a deletion request

Email us from the address connected to your Simple Dinners account. Use the subject line Delete My Simple Dinners Account and include the email address used to sign in.

Email deletion request

What will be deleted

  • Your Simple Dinners account and authentication profile.
  • Your household membership and account-level cloud records.
  • Cloud-synced information that is associated only with your account.

We may contact you to verify account ownership. If you own a shared household, tell us whether you also want that household deleted or need ownership handled first.

Shared household information

Information that was shared with a household may also belong to or be needed by other household members. Some shared content may remain available to those members where necessary to preserve their shared plans, lists, or recipes. We will explain any such limitation when processing your request.

Subscriptions must be canceled separately

Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Simple Dinners Plus subscription. Cancel it through the store where you purchased it.

Manage Apple subscriptions Manage Google Play subscriptions

Processing your request

We will confirm your request and provide an expected completion timeframe. Limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, billing disputes, legal compliance, or to protect the rights of other household members.
