Move the recipes you want to keep

Before the closure, we tested public Mealime recipe links on Android and share-sheet import on an iPad. These steps apply to recipe pages that remain publicly accessible.

1

Open a saved recipe link

Open a public recipe link you saved in your browser, messages, or bookmarks. The page must still load; an unavailable source cannot be imported through its link.

2

Share or copy the link

On iPhone, iPad, or Android, tap Share and choose Simple Dinners if offered. If the app isn't shown, copy the public recipe URL. You can paste it into the Simple Dinners Cookbook instead.

3

Import and check

If you copied the link, open Simple Dinners → Cookbook and import the URL. Review the ingredients, steps, and servings before saving. Repeat for each recipe.

Important: This is not a bulk migration or an account connection. Each public recipe is imported individually. Private notes, personal ingredient swaps, saved plans, and other account-only information do not transfer through a recipe link. Use any personal copies you saved separately.

Questions before you switch?

Where are Mealime's recipes now?

Mealime says its recipe catalog continues through Meals Hub in Albertsons Companies' grocery apps and websites. This does not transfer personal account data. A link import into Simple Dinners requires a publicly accessible recipe page.

Can Simple Dinners automatically import all my Mealime favorites?

Not automatically. Share or paste one publicly accessible Mealime recipe URL at a time. Before the closure, we tested three public recipes on Android, including a serving-size variation. We also confirmed that sharing a public Mealime recipe from Chrome on an iPad populated the recipe review screen. Import results can vary, so always check the recipe before cooking.

Will my private notes and customizations transfer?

No. Public recipe links don't contain your private notes, ingredient substitutions, meal plans, or account settings. If you saved personal copies before the closure, keep those separately; Simple Dinners cannot retrieve deleted Mealime account information.

Does Simple Dinners require a subscription?

No subscription is required for the core dinner-planning workflow, including the Cookbook, weekly planner, shopping list, and Cook Mode. Optional Plus features add extra convenience.
