Move the recipes you want to keep

We've tested public Mealime recipe links with Simple Dinners on Android. Here's the simplest way to try it yourself.

1

Open a Mealime recipe

Find a recipe you love in Mealime and open its public recipe page. Start with your favorites.

2

Share or copy the link

On Android, tap Share and choose Simple Dinners. If it isn't listed, copy the recipe's public URL instead.

3

Import and check

If you copied the link, open Simple Dinners → Cookbook and import the URL. Review the ingredients, steps, and servings before saving. Repeat for each recipe.

Important: This is not a bulk migration or an account connection. Each public recipe is imported individually. Private notes, personal ingredient swaps, saved plans, and other account-only information won't transfer through a recipe link. Save those separately.

Questions before you switch?

Will Mealime recipes disappear?

No. Mealime says its recipe catalog will continue through Meals Hub in Albertsons Companies' grocery apps and websites. But Mealime says personal data will be deleted when the old app closes.

Can Simple Dinners automatically import all my Mealime favorites?

Not automatically. Share or paste one publicly accessible Mealime recipe URL at a time. We tested three public recipes on Android, including another serving-size variation. Import results can vary, so always check the recipe before cooking.

Will my private notes and customizations transfer?

No. Public recipe links don't contain your private notes, ingredient substitutions, meal plans, or account settings. Make a separate copy of anything personal before shutdown.

Does Simple Dinners require a subscription?

No subscription is required for the core dinner-planning workflow, including the Cookbook, weekly planner, shopping list, and Cook Mode. Optional Plus features add extra convenience.
