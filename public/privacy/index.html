Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Simple Dinners handles information when you use the mobile app, web app, account features, recipe-import tools, household sharing, and Simple Dinners Plus.

Effective: July 24, 2026

1. Information stored on your device

Simple Dinners can store meal plans, recipes, pantry items, shopping lists, preferences, and other app information locally on your device. Free and local features can remain usable without cloud sharing.

2. Account and household information

When you create or use a Simple Dinners account, we process information needed to provide the account, including your email address, account identifier, authentication information, and basic account status.

When you create or join a household, cloud-synced information may include the household name, invite code, household membership and roles, shared weekly plans, shopping lists, Cookbook items, and related synchronization records.

3. Recipe-import information

When you use recipe-import features, the information you choose to submit may be sent to our processing services. This can include recipe links, pasted text, social-post links, captions, screenshots, images, or videos. We use that content to locate, extract, organize, and return recipe information to you.

Imported content may be processed by hosting, database, extraction, and artificial-intelligence service providers acting on our behalf. Limited technical logs may also be created for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

4. Subscription information

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process purchases for Simple Dinners Plus. We may receive information such as the product purchased, subscription status, store, transaction identifiers, renewal or expiration status, and related technical identifiers. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

5. Technical information

Our service providers may process limited technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as IP address, device or app information, timestamps, request details, and error logs.

Simple Dinners does not currently use advertising SDKs or dedicated analytics platforms, and we do not sell personal information.

6. How information is used

  • Provide accounts, authentication, and household sharing.
  • Synchronize shared plans, lists, and recipes.
  • Process recipe imports and organize submitted recipe content.
  • Confirm and restore Simple Dinners Plus access.
  • Maintain security, reliability, support, and troubleshooting.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

7. Service providers and disclosures

We share information only as needed to operate Simple Dinners, including with providers that support authentication and cloud storage, app hosting and recipe processing, artificial-intelligence processing, subscription management, and Apple or Google billing.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

8. Data retention and security

Local information remains on your device until you remove it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Account and cloud-synced information is kept while your account or household needs it, unless deletion is requested or retention is required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Simple Dinners is designed to help families, but its account and subscription features are intended to be managed by adults. We do not knowingly seek personal information directly from children.

10. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your Simple Dinners account and associated cloud data. Deleting the app or your Simple Dinners account does not automatically cancel an Apple or Google subscription.
